# 🎉 Fishtank TUI v0.11.0 - "Achievement Unlocked" Update

**Release Date:** February 17, 2026

## 🆕 What's New

### 🏆 Achievement System
Unlock 13 unique achievements by hitting major milestones:
- **First Fry** - Breed your first baby fish
- **Entrepreneur I/II/III** - Earn $100, $500, $1000
- **Aquarist I/II/III** - Raise 10, 25, 50 total fish
- **Janitor** - Clean tank 100 times
- **Decorator** - Place 10 decorations
- **Veteran/Dedicated** - Keep tank alive for 24h/48h
- **Fully Equipped** - Buy all equipment
- **Gardener** - Grow corner plants to max height

**Press `[A]` to view achievements and your lifetime stats!**

### 🎲 Random Events
Your tank is now more dynamic with surprise events (~1 per 100 minutes):

**Good Events:**
- 🎁 **Mystery Delivery** - Free decoration appears
- ⭐ **Lucky Day** - 2x income for 2 minutes
- 🐣 **Surprise Fry** - Random baby fish spawns
- 🐌 **Algae Eater** - Removes 50% of algae

**Bad Events:**
- ⚡ **Power Surge** - Equipment breaks (need to rebuy)
- 🦠 **Disease** - All fish lose 20 health
- ☁️ **Cloudy Water** - Purity drops by 30

### 📊 Lifetime Statistics
Track your aquarium journey:
- Total fish bred
- Total money earned
- Times cleaned
- Total playtime

### 🌱 Growing Corner Plants
- Corner plants now grow over time (max 8 levels)
- Creates "trees of Ys" stacking upward
- Grows faster if you own the Plants equipment
- ~1 level per 2 minutes

## 🔧 Improvements

- Stats now properly tracked across all actions
- Money tracking includes all income sources
- Achievement notifications appear when unlocked
- Lucky Day event adds visual feedback with 2x income

## 🎯 Updated Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `A` | **NEW:** View Achievements & Stats |
| `P` / `$` | Shop (Buy equipment/decorations) |
| `S` | Scrub Algae (Earn money) |
| `N` | Add new fish |
| `F` | Feed all fish |
| `W` | Water change |
| `X` | Remove decoration |
| `T` | Theme switcher |
| `Z` | Freeze/Unfreeze |
| `Q` | Quit & save |
